# Pathfinder 2E Encounters - Undo and Redo

The game records every change in an encounter, and the GM can undo and redo them one at a time. Nothing is rerolled: redoing a change brings back exactly what happened.

`encounter/undo`: Undoes the last change in the encounter. Only the GM can use this.
`encounter/redo`: Redoes the last change you undid. Any new change clears the changes you could redo.
`encounter/history [<encounter ID>]`: Lists every change in the encounter, and marks where undo and redo would take it.
%t **Note:** Once an encounter ends, its history can't be changed.
