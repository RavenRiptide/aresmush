# Advancement - Familiars

When you gain a familiar or pet while advancing, `advance/review` shows its details, and you can't finish the level with `advance/done` until you set its name, species, and form, and make any choices it has. When you finish the level, an approval request opens for staff to review your familiar. Until staff approve it, only its spells (a witch's spells) work, and you can't advance again.

For more information about familiars and pets, see `help familiars`.

## Details

`familiar/name <name>`: Sets your familiar's name.
`familiar/species <species>`: Sets what kind of creature your familiar is.
`familiar/form <form>`: Sets whether your familiar lives on land (`land`) or in water (`aquatic`).
`familiar/specific <specific familiar>`: Makes your familiar a specific familiar, or an ordinary one again with `familiar/specific none`.
`familiar/choose <choice>=<option>`: Makes a choice your specific familiar or leshy familiar has.
`familiar/pet <ability>, <ability>`: Chooses your pet's two abilities.
%t **Note:** For the rules on names and species, and the specific familiars and their choices, see `help chargen_familiar`.
%t **Note:** If your familiar came from Witch Dedication or the Witch class, staff approve your patron in the same request. Describe your patron in it with the Patron Template on [Patrons](/wiki/mechanics:patrons).

## Changing Approved Details

After staff approve your familiar, a change to its details opens a new approval request. Your familiar keeps its approved details until staff approve the change.

## Witch Dedication

When you take Witch Dedication and already have a familiar, the familiar your patron sends you becomes your familiar, and it waits for approval again with its old details to start from. If you chose the feat that gave you your old familiar, such as Pet, Familiar, Animal Accomplice, or Rat Familiar, you retrain that feat in the same level. For more information about retraining a feat, see `help advanceretrain`.
