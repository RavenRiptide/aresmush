# Pathfinder 2E Encounters - Battle Forms

In a battle form, the form's attacks are the only ones you can use, and a form that lists no attacks, such as *pest form*, can't make Strikes. `encounter/csheet` shows the attacks you have.

While you're in a battle form, the game refuses these, unless the GM adds `/force` (for more information, see `help e/as`):

- Casting spells.
- Manipulate actions, unless the form has hands.
- Using, equipping, or unequipping your gear, including a shield. Your gear's constant abilities still work.

`encounter/act dragon breath=<target>,<target>`: Breathes your dragon's damage on the targets you specify, while you're in *dragon form*. Each target rolls a basic save against your spell DC. You must wait 1d4 rounds before you use it again.
`encounter/act dismiss/<effect>`: Ends your form, if the form allows it. Otherwise, ask the GM to end it.

For more information about taking a shape with Untamed Form, see `help cast` or [read it on the web](/help/cast).
