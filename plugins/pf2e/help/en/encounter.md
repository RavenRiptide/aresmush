# Pathfinder 2E Encounters

An encounter tracks turns, actions, and hit points in a scene, such as during a fight. To take part in one, join the scene and participate by posing.

`e`, `init`, and `initiative` are all short for `encounter`: `e/view` is the same as `encounter/view`.

Each combatant has an ID, such as `#3`, which `encounter/view` shows. An encounter never reuses an ID. Any command that targets someone takes the combatant's ID, or their name if no one else has the same name.

## Taking part

- `help e/join`: Joining an encounter, and seeing it, your sheet, and other combatants.
- `help e/strike`: Making a Strike, throwing a bomb, and reloading.
- `help e/act`: Using an action, such as Trip, Demoralize, or Raise a Shield.
- `help e/cast`: Casting a spell.
- `help e/details`: Details you add to a command, such as `flanking` or `range 2`.
- `help e/turn`: Your turn, the modifiers on your last roll, finding the right command, and auras.
- `help e/gear`: Using the items you carry.
- `help e/cover`: Cover, concealment, and hiding.
- `help e/conditions`: Being grabbed, and conditions that limit what you can do.

## Actions that need more command syntax

- `help e/basic`: Basic actions, such as Delay, Sustain, Dismiss, and Administer First Aid.
- `help e/skillfeats`: General and skill feats, such as Battle Medicine and Shield Block.
- `help e/classfeats`: Class and ancestry feats, such as Flurry of Blows, Quick Draw, and Reactive Strike.
- `help e/forms`: Acting in a battle form.

## Running an encounter

GMs, start with `help e/gm` or [read it on the web](/help/encounter_gm).

## Other help

- `help effects`: Bonuses and penalties that last a while, such as Bless. [Read it on the web](/help/pf2e_effects).
- `help heal`: Healing, damage, and setting conditions. [Read it on the web](/help/pf2e_healing).
- `help hero points`: Rerolling a check, or avoiding death. [Read it on the web](/help/pf2e_hero_points).
