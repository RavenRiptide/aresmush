# Pathfinder 2E Encounters - Actions

`encounter/act <action>[=<target>][/<detail>...]`: Uses an action. An action that rolls a check, such as Trip, Demoralize, or Grapple, rolls it against the target's defense and applies the result. An action that affects you, such as Rage, Take Cover, or a stance, applies its effect to you. The game announces any other action to the room.
%t **Examples**:
%t `encounter/act trip=#3`
%t `encounter/act demoralize=#3/unintelligible`
%t `encounter/act raise a shield`
%t `encounter/act aid=bram/athletics`
%t `encounter/act balance/18`
%t **Note:** For an action with no target, add the DC as a number, as in `balance/18`. For more information about other command details, see `help e/details`.
%t **Note:** Not sure what to type? `encounter/how <name>` gives you the command. See `help e/turn` for more information.

Some actions need more than a target, or have their own command:

- `help e/basic`: Delay, Sustain, Dismiss, Escape, Administer First Aid, Assisted Recovery, and Retch.
- `help e/skillfeats`: Battle Medicine, Bon Mot, Shield Block, and Titan Wrestler.
- `help e/classfeats`: Feat actions that Strike, such as Flurry of Blows, and reactions such as Reactive Strike and Nimble Dodge.
- `help e/forms`: Acting in a battle form, and Dragon Breath.
- `help e/cover`: Seek, Hide, Sneak, Create a Diversion, and Recall Knowledge.
