# Familiar Abilities for the Day

During your daily preparations, you choose your familiar's abilities for the day: familiar abilities, which it has, and master abilities, which you have. How many it can have depends on your class and feats.

For more information about familiars and pets, see `help familiars`.

## Commands

`prepare/familiar`: Shows the abilities your familiar has and the ones it can have.
`prepare/familiar <ability>, <ability>`: Chooses your familiar's abilities for the day, such as `prepare/familiar Darkvision, Skilled (Arcana), Resistance (fire, cold)`. Put a choice in parentheses, and separate the abilities with commas.
`unprepare/familiar`: Takes back the abilities you chose since your last rest.
%t **Note:** Your choice is locked until your next rest. After the rest, your familiar keeps the same abilities until you choose others, as long as it can still have them.
%t **Note:** Your familiar can't choose abilities until staff approve it. A pet from the Pet feat keeps its two approved abilities and doesn't choose any.
%t **Note:** A specific familiar uses some of your familiar's abilities each day, so you choose only the rest.
%t **Note:** Spell Battery gives you one more spell slot, 3 ranks lower than your highest-rank slot. Cantrip Connection gives a prepared caster one more cantrip slot. A prepared caster fills them with `prepare` as usual. Like your other spell slots, they count from your next rest.
%t **Note:** If you have a repertoire, specify the cantrip that Cantrip Connection adds in parentheses, such as `prepare/familiar Cantrip Connection (Shield)`. The game keeps that cantrip on later days, so you can choose `Cantrip Connection` without it. Only staff can change it.
%t **Note:** For Spellcasting, specify a spell in your repertoire or prepared today, at least 5 ranks lower than your highest-rank spell slot, such as `prepare/familiar Spellcasting (Fear)`.
%t **Note:** Extra Alchemy lets you make one more item with `alchemy/prepare`, and Extra Vial gives you one more versatile vial at each rest. Gathering reagents still refills your vials only up to your usual number.
%t **Note:** A poppet can spend one of its abilities to be reinforced, which removes its weakness to fire for the day. Choose it as `Reinforced`, such as `prepare/familiar Reinforced, Darkvision`.
%t **Note:** An aeon wyrd's Aeon Stone Reservoir houses one of your aeon stones. Invest the stone with `invest` as usual, and it doesn't count toward your limit of invested items. A stone's resonant power doesn't work yet.
