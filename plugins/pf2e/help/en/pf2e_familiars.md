# Familiars and Pets

A familiar is a pet with special abilities. In Emblem of Ea, a character can have only one familiar or pet, so you can't take a feat that would give you a second one. Taking Witch Dedication is the exception: the familiar your patron sends you becomes your familiar.

Staff approve your familiar's name, species, and form, and any choices it has, before it can do anything but cast its spells (a witch's spells).

## Commands

`sheet/familiar`: Shows your familiar's or pet's sheet: its details, statistics, and abilities for the day. Until staff approve your familiar, it shows what's waiting for approval.
`sheet/familiar <character>`: Shows another character's familiar or pet, such as `sheet/familiar Morwen`. Like every sheet, a familiar's sheet is open to anyone.

## More Help

- `help chargen_familiar`: Setting up your familiar's name, species, form, and choices in chargen.
- `help advance_familiar`: Gaining a familiar while advancing, changing its details after approval, and Witch Dedication taking over a familiar.
- `help prepare_familiar`: Choosing your familiar's abilities for the day.
- `help encounter_familiar`: Bringing your familiar into an encounter, Commanding it, and its abilities there.
