# Preparing Spells as a Cleric

## Divine font slots

Your divine font gives you extra slots at your highest spell rank, which can contain only your font spell, heal or harm. To fill them, prepare your font spell once for each slot, such as `prepare cleric/1 = heal`. `prepared` shows how many font slots you have and how many you've filled. For more information about choosing your font, see `help dfont`.

## Deity spells

A cleric can prepare their deity's cleric spells as if they were on the divine list, even ones from another tradition or uncommon ones. `magic` lists them under your Divine Font.

## Restorative Channel

With Restorative Channel and a healing font, cast cleanse affliction, clear mind, sound body, or sure footing as usual without preparing it. It uses up one heal left at your font's rank, and it's cast at that rank.

## Miraculous Possibility

With Miraculous Possibility, you can hold one slot of rank 3 or higher open. When you cast from it, choose any divine spell you could prepare of 2 or more ranks lower than the slot. The spell is cast as a spell 2 ranks lower than the slot. It takes effect when you next `rest`.

`prepare/miraculous <rank>`: Holds a slot of `<rank>` open.
`unprepare/miraculous`: Prepares that slot as usual again.

To cast from the open slot, cast a spell you haven't prepared anywhere else as usual.
